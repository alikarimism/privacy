# Privacy Policy for Ali Karimi Personal Integration

**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how the personal integration application ("Application") owned and operated by Ali Karimi interacts with, accesses, and protects user information. This Application is designed for direct automation, messaging, and content management on authorized Instagram and Facebook accounts.

## 2. Information Accessed and Processed
The Application only accesses data through official Meta Graph APIs with permissions explicitly granted by the account administrator:
- **Public Profile Information:** Basic account details such as username, account ID, profile picture, follower and following counts.
- **Content and Media Metrics:** Uploaded posts, stories, reels, comments, and engagement analytics.
- **Direct Messaging Data:** Direct messages and conversation threads received on designated managed business accounts, used solely to enable automated and agentic replies.

## 3. Use of Information
Any information accessed is processed locally and used exclusively for:
- Viewing account analytics and publishing scheduled content.
- Responding to direct inquiries and managing message workflows.
- Facilitating direct account management by the account owner.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data or communications to any third parties or advertisers.

## 4. Data Storage and Retention
- Authentication tokens and configuration parameters are stored locally in secured, encrypted environments.
- Direct message content and user data are not permanently retained on external databases or third-party servers.

## 5. User Rights and Data Deletion
Users may request information about stored data, request data deletion, or revoke the Application's access at any time:
- **Revoking Access:** You can revoke access directly via your Instagram or Facebook Settings under **Apps and Websites**.
- **Data Deletion Request:** Contact us at **alikarimidvj@gmail.com** or send a direct message on Instagram to **@realalikarimi**. All associated temporary data will be deleted within 24 hours of receiving the request.
